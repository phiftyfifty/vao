How it works

  • Learn is a path of short lessons. Each unit is one of your class decks (L1 Greetings through L2 Expressions, plus the everyday phrases and your class notes), split into lessons of about six words.
  • A lesson introduces each word, then drills it several ways: listen or read and pick the meaning, pick the Lao script, match pairs, build the phrase from tiles, and say it out loud while recording yourself to compare.
  • A wrong answer comes back at the end of the lesson, and the word goes into Review. That's where spaced repetition (the Anki part) brings words back on a schedule.
  • Play is a river-crossing game: hop onto the lily pad with the right Lao word before the timer runs out. Sometimes you hear the word instead of reading it. Wrong pads sink, and missed words go to Review.
  • The ກ + abc pill switches romanization on or off, so you can wean yourself onto the script.
