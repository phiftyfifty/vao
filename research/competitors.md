# Lao learning apps

Checked against live pages, not from memory.

## Duolingo

No Lao course.
`duolingo.com/course/lao/learn-lao` redirects to the homepage in a real browser, and Lao is missing from the courses list.
The URL looks real in search results only because the site answers 200 for any slug, including made-up ones.

## Bhasaly (bhasaly.com/learn-lao)

The closest thing to Vao.
Browser-based, 370 short tap-based lessons across 12 levels, taught in Lao script, with recorded audio on every word and an alphabet course.
No account needed, optional email login to sync.
It's a general course aimed at anyone, with no heritage or family angle.

## Ling (ling-app.com)

Gamified, freemium course across many less-common languages, Lao among them.
Breadth over depth, the same engine for every language.
Ranks for "learn Lao" and runs SEO posts like "No Lao on Duolingo?".

## LingFast (App Store)

iOS app built on AI: a TikTok-style feed of Lao dialogs and news, on-device Whisper shadowing with a 0 to 100 pronunciation score, an AI chat tutor, and anonymous live voice calls with other learners.
Freemium with in-app purchases.
Strong on speaking tech, but generic content and an indie developer.

## Simply Learn Lao

Phrasebook app for travelers with phonetic plus script and audio.
Not a course.

## LaoPod101

Showed up in searches through ThaiPod101 reviews only.
I didn't confirm a current Lao course.

## What this means for Vao

"The Lao course the big apps won't make" no longer works as a pitch, because Bhasaly already ships a solid general course with real audio.
What nobody does is the heritage angle: speaking with your own family, family-recorded audio, and the home and temple vocabulary that comes with it.
That's the positioning on the draft landing page.

## Audience size

About 245,000 people in the US identified as Laotian in 2023, about 180,000 of them Laotian alone (Pew, from ACS 2021-23).
Lao New Year (Pi Mai) in mid April has festivals in several US cities, including Houston and Tennessee in 2026.
